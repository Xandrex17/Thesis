Comments and Suggestions
Problem / Research Gap

The problem is clear: AIS data may be incomplete or intentionally turned off, while SAR can detect vessels independently. The main challenge is deciding whether a SAR-detected vessel truly has no matching AIS record or whether the mismatch is only caused by transmission gaps, timing, congestion, or nearby vessels.

Make this the main problem of the study and avoid making the background too broad.

Computer Science / Computational Contribution

The topic is appropriate for BS Computer Science because it involves graph modeling, spatiotemporal data, multimodal data fusion, anomaly detection, and machine learning.

Be very clear about what you are developing yourselves. Is the main contribution the graph model, the AIS-SAR matching algorithm, the anomaly detector, or the complete framework?

The thesis should not become only an integration of existing AIS and SAR tools.

Alignment of the Problem and Proposed Solution

Your three stated problems are about AIS-SAR matching, distinguishing real discrepancies from temporary gaps, and reducing false positives.

Your proposed framework should therefore directly show how it will solve each of these.

Make the flow clear:
AIS trajectories + SAR detections → spatial/temporal matching → graph representation → discrepancy/anomaly analysis → dark-vessel candidate.

Dark-Vessel Detection

Be careful with the term “dark vessel.” A vessel with no matching AIS record is not automatically intentionally hiding.

I suggest using “dark-vessel candidate” or “AIS-SAR discrepancy” until the system has enough evidence to classify it. You already recognize this limitation in the background, so keep the terminology consistent.

Graph Framework

Explain why a spatiotemporal graph is needed instead of a simpler AIS-SAR matching method.

Specify what will become the nodes, edges, and features of the graph. For example: vessel positions, speed, course, time difference, distance, and relationships with nearby vessels.

This is important because this will show the real computational depth of the study.

POC

Your POC should clearly demonstrate the proposed computational framework.

It may show the SAR-detected vessel, possible AIS matches, matching/confidence result, detected discrepancy, and reason why the vessel was flagged.

The POC should support the thesis algorithm, not become the main contribution.

Objectives

When you finalize the objectives, make sure each one directly matches the three problems you identified.

Include measurable evaluation such as matching accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and false-positive rate, especially because reducing false positives is one of your stated problems.
